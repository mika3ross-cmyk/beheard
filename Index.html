Here's what's actually going on.

When someone gets defensive, their brain shifts into reverse.

They can't hear you. They can't problem-solve. They can't move forward. You could say the sky is blue and they'd find a way to disagree.

Most people spend years trying to change the other person. Their tone. Their defensiveness. Their intent.

You can work on that forever and get nowhere.

What actually works? Changing what you're willing to participate in.

The Program

A 30-day challenge. 20 sessions. Learn it. Try it. Bring it back.

Communication basics no one ever taught you.

Days 2–15 — 14 Weekday Sessions  ·  Sessions delivered Monday–Friday

Relationship rules + exactly what to say.

This is the IF THIS, THEN THAT part. Every session I pull up one specific situation — the kind you've been avoiding, fumbling, or losing sleep over — break down what's actually happening, and hand you the exact words. Not general advice. Not "try to stay calm." The timing. The delivery. What to do when they get defensive anyway.

  • ·What to say when you've already said it ten times and nothing changes
  • ·What to say when someone interrupts you and you freeze
  • ·What to say when an apology isn't actually an apology
  • ·What to say when you want to walk away but you don't know how
  • ·What to say when you're being guilt-tripped and you know it
  • ·What to say when you need something and you're scared to ask
  • ·And more — because there are a lot of situations and you probably have most of them

Each session is short enough to listen to on your commute, your walk, or instead of doom-scrolling — check your screen time, I'll wait. Together they build something — a relational framework for knowing your worth and acting like it, brick by brick, in the relationships that matter most.

Every session ends with a challenge. Something small. Something actionable. Something uncomfortable. Try it on a real person before the next one — and share what happened. Best breakthroughs get rewarded.

By the end of day 15 you've put in real reps with real people — you'll see and feel a difference.

Days 16–20 — Your Situations, Decoded

Now bring it back.

You've done the reps. Something happened — maybe it went well, maybe you froze or blew up again anyway and left feeling worse, not better. This is where we debrief and make sense of your actual situation — what happened, what you said, what they said, where it went sideways. I'll select scenarios and get on Zoom with you. We walk through exactly what was happening, what to do differently, and what to say next time.

The audio gets shared with the group (with identifying details removed) so everyone learns from it. Because your situation? Probably someone else's situation too.

This is the part that makes this unlike anything else at this price. You're not just watching someone else's coaching. You might be in it.

A note on how I work.

Therapy will teach you to love yourself.

But loving yourself doesn't happen in a vacuum — and we're never taught what that looks and sounds like in relationship with other people. I found that despite a lot of work on myself, there was a gap in skills I just didn't get from traditional therapy or training. I still didn't know what to say or do when someone was talking over me, or how to say the thing I'd been sitting on for six months.

At some point you have to take loving yourself out into the wild. Into your actual relationships. With your actual people. And that's where most of us realize — insight isn't enough.

I didn't start getting different results in my relationships until I started doing things differently. Not thinking differently. Not feeling differently. Doing. Moving through hard conversations in an inarguable way that communicated that I matter — out loud, in real time, with the people who push my buttons the most.

That's what I teach. Not how to love yourself. How to love yourself out loud.

I'm both a therapist and a relationship coach — and I think relationships need both. Therapy helps you understand why you do what you do. Coaching gives you something to do with that understanding. Most people have plenty of insight. What they're missing is knowing what it looks and sounds like in real life — with real people, in real time.

Questions.

Is this for couples, or can I do this alone?

Either. You don't need your partner, your teenager, or your mother-in-law to sign up with you. This is about what YOU can understand and do differently. That alone changes the dynamic — even if the other person never watches a single video.

Will this fix my partner / parent / teenager?

Not directly — but changing what you do changes the dynamic. And you'll leave knowing exactly what's yours to work on and what isn't. That clarity alone is worth the price of admission.

How much time does this take each day?

5–30 minutes per day, weekdays only. The foundation and script sessions run shorter. The scenario walkthroughs in week 4 run a bit longer because they're real situations with real nuance. Nothing requires homework. You watch, you absorb, you try something.

What if I miss a day?

Everything lives in your private portal. Watch at whatever pace works for you. Lifetime access means you can come back when you hit a rough patch six months from now and pull exactly what you need.

Will my scenario definitely get picked?

I can't guarantee every submission gets its own walkthrough — but I pick the ones that will teach the most to the most people. Even if your exact scenario isn't chosen, you'll almost certainly watch someone else's and think "that's exactly what I needed."

Is this therapy?

No. This is education and coaching. I'm a licensed therapist drawing on 20+ years of clinical experience to give you practical tools — but this program isn't a substitute for therapy, and it doesn't create a therapeutic relationship between us.
